# Elegant Design Language

Principles reference — palettes and fonts are generated fresh per project (brand-designer color-wheel rules, fonts skill). Nothing here is a menu to copy.

## Aliases
Luxury, Sophisticated, Premium, Refined, High-end, Upscale

## Attitude

Quiet luxury — premium quality conveyed through restraint, proportion, and exquisite detail. Whispers rather than shouts: the confidence to leave space empty, set type light, and let photography carry the story. Timeless over trendy; aspirational yet warm.

## Principles

- Light type weights (300–500, rarely 700+) with slightly opened letter-spacing — refinement lives in the details
- Extremely generous space: tall sections, wide margins, one idea at a time
- Photography-led where assets exist — large, professional, editorial; the design frames it and gets out of the way
- Motion slow and smooth (400–600ms) — rushed transitions cheapen everything
- Components nearly invisible: thin 1px borders or the faintest fills, minimal radius, no heavy shadows
- All-caps only in small doses with generous tracking (0.05–0.1em)

## Dial Presets

VARIANCE 5–7 · MOTION 3–5 · DENSITY 1–3
DENSITY at the floor is the point — cramped and luxury cannot coexist. Push VARIANCE to 7 with asymmetric editorial layouts for fashion/creative luxury.

## Palette Strategy

Muted and warm-leaning: soft blacks or espresso darks, warm whites, one metallic or earthen accent (gold, bronze, camel, taupe — vary which per project, and consider a cool register with stone grays for modern luxury). Complementary scheme at low saturation. Saturated brights are banned; the accent appears rarely enough to feel like a gilt edge.

## Typography Strategy

Serif-led: a high-contrast or literary serif display (light weights, large sizes) over a refined sans or serif body at 17–18px with 1.7–1.8 line-height. The serif choice defines the brand's era — didone reads fashion, old-style reads heritage, contemporary serif reads modern luxury; choose per project.

## Watch Out

- The beige + gold + espresso combo is the premium-brand cliché — vary temperature and accent per project
- Bold heavy type breaks the register instantly; so do star ratings, badges, and busy cards
- Low-quality or stocky photography destroys the entire effect — prefer fewer, better images or none
- Slow ≠ sluggish: transitions are unhurried, page load is instant
