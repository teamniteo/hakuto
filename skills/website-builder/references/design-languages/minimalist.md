# Minimalist Design Language

Principles reference — palettes and fonts are generated fresh per project (brand-designer color-wheel rules, fonts skill). Nothing here is a menu to copy.

## Aliases
Minimal, Clean, Simple, Whitespace, Typography-focused

## Attitude

Less is more. Content and typography ARE the design; space does the work decoration would. A minimalist site earns attention through what it leaves out — every element that remains must justify itself. Confident, quiet, precise.

## Principles

- Typography is the primary hierarchy tool — scale and weight contrast replace boxes, borders, and color coding
- Whitespace is generous and deliberate: more space between sections than within, related elements grouped tight
- One accent color, used strategically; everything else is neutral
- Interactions subtle and fast — enhance usability, never perform
- Clean geometric shapes; no decorative elements, no ornamental gradients
- Either subtle fills OR borders for components — pick one system, never both

## Dial Presets

VARIANCE 4–6 · MOTION 2–4 · DENSITY 2–4
Push DENSITY down (2) and VARIANCE toward 6 for editorial/portfolio work; keep VARIANCE 4–5 for SaaS where clarity leads.

## Palette Strategy

Monochromatic or near-monochromatic: a neutral ramp (warm, cool, or pure — vary the temperature per project) plus one accent. Complementary scheme when an accent contrast is needed. The accent can be any hue — a warm brown reads artisan, a cobalt reads product, a deep green reads calm; don't default to blue. Background is near-white or near-black, never gray mush.

## Typography Strategy

One distinctive font used decisively, or a literary serif display over a geometric sans body. Hierarchy comes from hard contrast: light body (300–400) against heavy or expressive display, ≥3× size jumps. Max 2–3 weights total. A timid type scale is the fastest way to make minimalism read as "unfinished" instead of "intentional."

## Watch Out

- Minimal ≠ empty: if the page reads generic, the typography isn't working hard enough
- Don't mix border-components and fill-components on one page
- Gray-on-gray low-contrast text is not sophistication, it's illegibility
- Resist adding "just one card grid" of three equal boxes — that's the default this language exists to avoid
